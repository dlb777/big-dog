Big Dog Smokehouse & BBQ

Local, Fresh and Experienced!

Bow Valley Gourmet is proud and excited to announce the opening of our Food Truck (July 2021) Big Dog Smokehouse and BBQ! Big Dog Smokehouse and BBQ with be offering regional Southern BBQ. We smoke our own meats with a variety of hardwoods. We have created our own rubs, make all of our sides and sauces from scratch.

This is a unique experience and another avenue for clients to enjoy a variety of foods offered by Bow Valley Gourmet. The exciting news about this Food Truck- it has a professional Chef!

Our Chef Michael Anderson will be offering Slow and Low Southern BBQ. With regional favorites from St. Louis style ribs, Pulled Pork Grilled Cheese, Smoked Turkey Tacos, Smashburgers and Maple smoked BBQ Chicken Sliders. There are also some original twists to the classic BBQ Menu. We will be sticking to the same concept that has made Bow Valley Gourmet a local favorite – We customize every Menu to fit the event. This is a Food Truck experience like no other in Canmore, Alberta.

Big Dog Smokehouse and BBQ is also available for Catering events such as Weddings, Concerts, Family re-unions, or groups that are camping. We will be able to drive to any location and offer customized Menus for your event.

Main Street · Canmore

2026 Main Street

We are very excited to announce that we are going to be back at our location in 2026 on Main Street in Canmore, Alberta.

Our location is 806 Main Street – right in the heart of Canmore.

This year we have added more tables, Cornhole Gardens and Live Music every weekend!

The Menu will be all of the highlights we have been working on and some new additions:

Gringo Taco, Brisket Smashburger, Tank’s Smokie and our summer favorite – Watermelon Salad! We have also add Pork Belly Burnt Ends and a Foot Long Hot Dog with our Bun being made by our partners at JK Bakery.

This summer we will also have Specials throughout the season such as Cuban Sliders, Hickory Smoked BBQ Ribs and Tot-Chos!

We are starting off with Wing Night every Wednesday! Swing by and grab #1 of our Smoked Wings, Baby Carrots and Ranch for $12.

Keep your eyes peeled on our social media pages for updates.
